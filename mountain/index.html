How to play

You build directly on the painting. Sculpt with the terrain brushes, plant or clear forest, then string lifts (bottom terminal first) and draw runs from top to bottom: they are cut through the forest and rated from their pitch. Rotate the view to reach the back side, or slide the camera to overhead for precision. Switch to Trail Map for the finished poster.
