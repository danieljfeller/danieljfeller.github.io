How to play

The map looks north: the base is at the bottom, the summit at the top. Sculpt with the terrain brushes, plant or clear forest, then string lifts (bottom terminal first) and draw runs from top to bottom. Difficulty is rated from the pitch automatically. Groom runs for corduroy, or leave them as moguls. Switch to Trail Map when you want to see the painting.
